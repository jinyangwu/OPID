Backbones
& \texttt{Qwen3-1.7B} for ALFWorld, WebShop, and Search; \texttt{Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct} for ScienceWorld. \\

Training update steps
& 160 updates for ALFWorld, ScienceWorld, and WebShop; 150 updates for Search. \\

Training batch size
& 16 for ALFWorld, ScienceWorld, and WebShop; 128 for Search. \\

Rollout group size $N$
& 8. \\

Actor optimizer and learning rate
& AdamW with learning rate $1\times10^{-6}$ and weight decay $0.01$. \\

PPO/GRPO clip parameter $\epsilon$
& $0.2$ for both lower and upper policy-ratio clipping. \\

Skill coefficient $\lambda_{\mathrm{skill}}$
& $0.001$ for episode-level skill teacher advantage and $0.001$ for critical-step skill teacher advantage; auxiliary OPD loss coefficient is 0. \\

KL regularization
& Low-variance KL loss on the actor. KL coefficient is 0.01 for ALFWorld, ScienceWorld, and WebShop, and 0.001 for Search; KL-in-reward is disabled. \\

Policy-update epochs / minibatches
& 1 policy epoch. PPO minibatch size is 256 for ALFWorld and ScienceWorld, 64 for WebShop, and 512 for Search; per-GPU microbatch size is 32, 32, 8, and 16 respectively. \\

Rollout decoding
& vLLM rollout with sampling enabled; temperature 0.4, top-$p$ 1.0, and top-$k$ -1. \\

Maximum prompt / response lengths
& Prompt length is 2,048 for ALFWorld and 4,096 for ScienceWorld, WebShop, and Search; response length is 512 for all tasks. \\

Maximum environment steps
& 30 for ALFWorld and ScienceWorld, 15 for WebShop, and 4 for Search. \\

Gradient clipping
& Global gradient norm clipped to 1.0. \\

Precision and distributed strategy
& bfloat16 rollout dtype with FSDP actor/ref/critic workers. Scripts use one node with 8 GPUs; vLLM tensor parallel size is 1 except WebShop, where it is 4. \\

Reward definition during training
& ALFWorld and ScienceWorld use sparse completion reward $10\cdot\mathbb{1}[\mathrm{success}]$; WebShop maps successful terminal episodes to 10 and otherwise 0; Search uses exact-match QA reward, 1 for a correct final answer and 0 otherwise. \\

Random seeds
& Environment seed 0. \\
